## Ordered recommendation and alternatives

**Recommended first bounded checkpoint:** candidate 1, a report-only deployment/runtime evidence plan after operator supplies supported Node/OS/filesystem targets. It addresses explicit proof limits across business persistence and infrastructure without changing contracts. If those target facts are not available, stop at discovery of unavailable evidence rather than running speculative environments.

**Alternative:** candidate 3, exact feature-mode characterization, if the owner prefers a repository-local, readily testable contract map. It must preserve the exact R7.8 gate distinctions and avoid feature redesign.

**Defer/no action:** retain current behavior and defer all implementation, M02 extraction, M12/M13 reclassification, log migration, API deprecation and data migration until the relevant product/operator/external-caller evidence exists. This is a valid outcome, not a failure to advance.

Candidate 2 is a strong prerequisite before any public surface removal; candidate 4 before diagnostics changes; candidate 5 before workflow ownership changes. No shortlist item overrides RC/FI approvals or accepted R7.5–R7.8 contracts. These priorities are proposals and are not owner selections.
